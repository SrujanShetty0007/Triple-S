How to calculate CGPA?

Step 1: Get SGPA for each semester

Step 2: Multiply each semester's SGPA with its credits

Step 3: Calculate the total of all (SGPA × Credits)

Step 4: Calculate the total credits of all semesters

Step 5: Divide the total sum by total credits

Formula: CGPA = ∑(SGPA × Credits) / ∑Credits
